Welcome to Suraj Public School

Suraj Public School, Kotkasim, is an institution where academic growth and personal growth move together. Our learning environment is designed to help students ask thoughtful questions, work with discipline and express their ideas with confidence.

Through a balanced emphasis on knowledge, values, creativity, technology awareness and responsible citizenship, we encourage every learner to discover a meaningful direction for the future.
