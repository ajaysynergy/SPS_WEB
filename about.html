Who we are

Learning that reaches beyond the textbook.

Suraj Public School is a CBSE affiliated senior secondary school in Kotkasim, Rajasthan. Since its establishment in 2003, the school has been a place where learning is connected with discipline, curiosity, respect and a growing sense of responsibility.

Our work is grounded in a simple belief: students flourish when they feel known, challenged and supported. We aim to provide a calm, purposeful environment in which academic development and character building are given equal importance.

Our journey

Steady progress, rooted in values.

The story of Suraj Public School is best understood as an ongoing commitment to education in the Kotkasim community. Our journey continues through everyday teaching, conversations with families, student effort and the shared responsibility of preparing young people for a changing world.
