Coding & robotics

Learning to think, make and improve.

Coding and robotics offer students a practical language for curiosity. They can break a challenge into steps, write instructions, observe what happens, find an error and try a better approach.

The programme is designed around hands-on learning in coding, programming, robotics, artificial intelligence awareness, logical thinking, problem solving, innovation and project-based learning.
